SuperBookmarks extension

Connect to the app

Open SuperBookmarks, go to Settings → Sources → Save from your browser, and enter the pairing code shown there.

Check mark on saved pages

Show a check mark on pages you've saved

Off until you turn it on. Turning it on lets the extension see the address of the page you're on and ask SuperBookmarks on this computer whether it's saved (your browser asks you to allow that). Addresses never leave this computer.

Posts on X, Instagram and TikTok

On a profile, feed or search page, click the toolbar button: the Save window lists the posts already loaded in your tab, and you tick the ones to save. That needs only the connection above: posts go to your SuperBookmarks and nowhere else.

Download button on each post

Off until you turn it on, one site at a time. The extension then puts a small SuperBookmarks button on each post you see on that site; a post is read only when you click its button. It never scrolls, opens posts or reads anything in the background, and it never uses your cookies or login.

For support
Add "Copy page structure" to the right-click menu

For when saving posts from a site stops working. On that site's page, right-click, choose it, and send what it shows to whoever is helping you. It has only what the page is built of: tag names, test ids, roles and class names. No text, links, pictures or names.

What the extension can do, and why

  • Read the page you're on, when you click its button (activeTab, scripting): the address and title, and on X, Instagram and TikTok the posts on the page, for the Save window.
  • Add "Save link to SuperBookmarks" to the right-click menu (contextMenus), and "Copy page structure" if you turn it on.
  • Remember the connection (storage), on this computer.
  • Talk to the app at 127.0.0.1 only: never to a website or a server of ours.
  • Only if you turn on the check mark: see the address of the page you're on (tabs).
  • Only for the sites you switch on: put a Download button on their posts (access to that site).

No access to your history, cookies or logins.
